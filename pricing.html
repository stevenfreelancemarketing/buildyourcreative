A FEW USEFUL ANSWERS

Before we build.

Is the $299 price the total build fee?+

Yes, for the defined Website Launch scope. Domain and hosting are separate. Extra work is quoted and agreed before it begins.

How quickly can my site launch?+

Timing depends on the content, scope, and feedback. We agree on a delivery schedule before work begins rather than promise the same timeline for every project.

Can I edit it myself?+

The base package includes the site files and handoff instructions, not a visual editing dashboard. You can ask us or another developer to update it. A client-facing editor can be scoped separately.

Do you only work with contractors?+

No. Trades and local service businesses are our focus, but other businesses are welcome to inquire.

Will the site guarantee leads or revenue?+

No. We build clear paths to inquiry and a stronger presentation of your services. Results depend on demand, traffic, your offer, and follow-up.
