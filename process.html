FROM FIRST HELLO TO HANDOFF
01

Start with your business.

A short inquiry gives us your services, location, and goals. If a conversation would help, request a free 20-minute Zoom consultation. A brief review beforehand helps make that conversation useful.

02

Agree on the right-sized project.

We clarify deliverables, content needs, timing, and payment. Website Launch is $299. Payment terms and your delivery schedule are confirmed in writing before work begins. Larger packages receive a custom scope.

03

Review a private preview.

See your site take shape before it goes live. Website Launch includes one consolidated revision round within the agreed scope.

04

Launch with a clear handoff.

After approval and final payment, we connect your domain, check the site, and deliver source files and instructions. Domain and hosting accounts remain under your control.

05

Keep the support you need.

Thirty days of limited support are included. After that, optional $50/month care covers minor updates and routine checks. Larger improvements can be quoted as needed.
